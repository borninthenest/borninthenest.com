The Nest

A gentle membership for your whole pregnancy.

The five lessons and the weekly letter will always be free. The Nest is for when you'd like a little more: a steady, loving companion matched to exactly where you are in your pregnancy.

What you'll find inside

  • A guide matched to your due date. Each week, what's happening for you and your baby, and one small way to prepare.
  • Audio practices. Calm birth breathing, relaxation for sleep, and quiet moments to connect with your baby.
  • Printable workbooks. Your birth preferences, questions for your midwife, a transfer plan you can feel calm about, and your home birth supply list.
  • A monthly live Q&A. Bring your questions and leave feeling lighter.
  • Member-only birth stories. Calm, real stories from home and birth center births, and births where plans changed and still felt good.
