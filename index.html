Home birth, honestly

A few myths, untangled.

MythHome birth is only for a certain kind of mom.

FactHome birth is for any mom who wants to feel calm and at home. Moms from all walks of life choose it.

MythYou don't have a say in where you give birth.

FactYou can choose wherever you want to give birth. It's your choice.

MythWithout a hospital, I'm not safe.

FactYour body has done this for centuries. And you're not alone: you have a team around you that has been with you for the past nine months and knows you and your baby inside and out. They'll know if something is off.

MythBirth is only about pain.

FactYes, there will be some pain, but birth isn't only about pain. Your body is made to do this. Labor comes in waves and builds up slowly, and between contractions you get real breaks to rest and breathe. Nature doesn't want you to suffer.

Why so many moms love giving birth at home

It's your own nest. It's not just your bed and your snacks. It's your food, your space, and everyone you love right around you. And after you give birth, you never have to leave.

The right midwife makes you feel calm, heard and loved, right from the first moment. That's how you should feel.

Born in the Nest

The birth story you'll want to tell.

With my first baby, I was scared. I thought I had to give birth in a hospital, and I had no idea what to expect. My labor lasted nearly two and a half days. Along the way I felt miserable and lost, because I had no control over what my body wanted to do. And after I gave birth, I was drained, but they still wouldn't feed me, and they kept asking me questions instead of letting me rest.

My second birth was a completely different story. I found the most amazing midwives, and that changed everything. I felt truly loved and cared for by these women, and they made me feel so confident that my body was ready to do this. I read a couple of books, practiced hypnobirthing, and met with them every month. Their words, their calm and their expertise prepared me for my home birth, and it became the most beautiful thing I could ever imagine.
