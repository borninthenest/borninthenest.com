The weekly letter

One short, loving letter every Sunday of your pregnancy, matched to your due month, all the way to the day you meet your baby. Each one has three little parts:

  1. One honest momentA real moment from my pregnancies, the sweet and the slightly ridiculous.
  2. One calm ideaSomething worth knowing for right where you are, explained gently.
  3. One small stepA tiny thing to try this week. Five minutes, tops. Snacks optional.

The five lessons aren't in the letter. They're free right here on the site, whenever you want them: read the five free lessons.
