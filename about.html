About

Hi, I'm Sybille

My mission: that every woman knows she has a choice in where and how she gives birth, and feels calm, loved and prepared, whatever she chooses.

I'm a mom of two. My first baby was born in a hospital with an epidural. I was completely numb, and I felt helpless. My second was born at home with a midwife I trusted, and that birth showed me how calm and beautiful giving birth can be. Same body, completely different birth. The biggest difference was where I was and how calm and loved I felt.

As my midwife Sarah always said, pregnant women need to feel loved.

That's what Born in the Nest is about.

My midwives felt a little like superheroes to me. They paid attention to everything: what I was eating, how I was sleeping, how I was really feeling, and the small things I'd never have thought to mention. Often they seemed to understand what I needed just by looking at me, before I said a word.

Why Born in the Nest exists

No one told me I had another option until my second pregnancy. I want you to know sooner.

Many women think home birth is only for the craziest people. I'd love to gently change that. 😄

Choosing where to give birth, and finding the right midwife or birth center, are big decisions, and I wish I'd had someone I could count on when I made them. When I was pregnant, just a message from one of my midwives could calm me down and make my whole day feel lighter. That's what I want Born in the Nest to be for you: a warm, trusted place you can come back to whenever you need it.

It isn't a one-time thing. The five short lessons are free right here on the site, for anyone, no signup needed. And if you'd like, I'll send you a weekly letter every Sunday, matched to your due month, the way a loving midwife would walk beside you. You're never alone on this journey. Think of it as a steady hand with you every week, all the way to the day you meet your baby.
